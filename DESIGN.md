# AquaGas Design System

## Direction

AquaGas uses a spacious, practical marketplace interface. It should feel local, trustworthy, and operationally clear rather than decorative. Desktop layouts use the available canvas for comparison and scanning; mobile layouts collapse naturally into touch-friendly single-column flows.

## Brand

- Aqua blue is the primary interaction and water-service color.
- Gas red/orange identifies LPG and selective urgency states.
- The existing AquaGas logo and split-color wordmark remain the primary signature.
- Neutral surfaces provide contrast for real provider imagery, product data, and status colors.

## Layout

- Customer pages use a `max-w-7xl` marketplace canvas with responsive horizontal padding.
- Store and order content should not remain constrained to a phone-width column on desktop.
- Repeated collections become responsive grids; task sequences remain readable vertical flows.
- Sticky controls are reserved for navigation, filtering, and category switching.
- Store chat belongs immediately after store contact information in order details.

## Components

- Store cards use large imagery, strong store names, two-line addresses, and comparable rating/ETA/fee metadata.
- Product cards use larger product imagery and expose stock, price, unit, and quantity controls together.
- Reviews and store details use focused overlays so customers can inspect supporting information without losing their place.
- AquaBot uses a larger responsive panel, quick recommendation prompts, voice input, live context, and cart actions.
- Controls maintain a minimum 44px touch target and visible keyboard focus.

## Responsive Behavior

- Phone: single column, horizontal category scrolling, bottom navigation, nearly full-width overlays.
- Tablet: two-column cards where content remains legible.
- Desktop: wider content canvas and multi-column comparison grids.
- Wide desktop: content remains bounded and centered rather than stretching indefinitely.

## Motion

Use short ease-out transitions for hover, opening, and status feedback. Avoid elastic or decorative motion; motion should clarify state and hierarchy.

## Content

Use real marketplace data for prices, ratings, delivery estimates, inventory, and availability. Do not fabricate promotional claims or delivery guarantees.
